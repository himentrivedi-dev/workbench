# Worked Example: Activity Search Leadership Update

**Status:** At risk  
**As of:** <date>

## Outcome

The team is improving activity discovery so travellers can find relevant experiences with fewer irrelevant results.

## Progress

- Retrieval flow and data dependencies have been mapped.
- The team is validating preference interpretation and hard-constraint behaviour.
- The next milestone is a labelled evaluation set and a measured baseline.

## Risk

Source metadata is incomplete for some activity attributes. This can reduce ranking quality and make explanations less reliable.

## Mitigation

Separate hard constraints from inferred preferences, identify missing source fields, and evaluate ranking against labelled examples before claiming quality improvement.

## Decision needed

Confirm the product owner for relevance definitions and agree the initial success measures.

## Confidence

The data gap is observed in the current assessment. The expected impact on ranking must be quantified through evaluation; no accuracy improvement is claimed yet.
